This policy explains what data Zero-Technologies Cloud ("the Service", at `cloud.zero-technologies.co.uk`) holds, why, and what rights you have. The data controller is {{ site.controller }} ("we", "us"). Contact: [{{ site.contact_email }}](mailto:{{ site.contact_email }}).

You sign in to the Service with your Zero-Technologies Sign-in account. What the sign-in service itself collects is covered by the [Sign-in Privacy Policy](/sign-in/privacy/).

## 1. Data we hold

**Your account**

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Username, display name and email address, from your sign-in account | To create and recognise your account and contact you about it |
| Your groups and Patreon tier, from your sign-in account | To decide whether you can use the Service and what storage quota you get |
| Profile details you choose to add (for example phone number, address, avatar) | To show them to other users, as you choose in your profile settings |
| Settings, such as language and notification choices | To run the Service the way you set it up |

**Your content**: files you upload, and the calendars, contacts, documents, whiteboards, comments, chat messages and shares you create. We hold these only to provide the Service to you and the people you share with. We do not look at, analyse or use your content for any other purpose, except where needed to keep the Service secure or working (for example, the virus check below) or where the law requires it.

**Automatic processing of your content**

- Every uploaded file is scanned for viruses by a scanner that runs on our own servers.
- Image and video previews are generated on our servers. The Photos app can read the date and location stored inside your photos to organise them.

**Activity and usage**: a history of actions on your files and shares (who created, changed, shared or deleted what, and when), shown to you in the Activity feed.

**Logs**: your IP address, browser, the pages and files you request, and sign-in attempts, recorded by the Service and by our network and security systems.

## 2. Legal basis

We process account data, your content and activity history to provide the Service you asked to use (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)), and logs on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the Service secure and working (Article 6(1)(f)).

## 3. How long we keep it

- **Account data and content** are kept while your account exists. When you delete a file it moves to your trash and is removed after 30 days, or sooner if you empty the trash or you're running out of space. Older versions of files are kept for up to 90 days.
- **Activity history** is deleted after 365 days.
- **Logs**: network and access logs are deleted after about 2–3 days, and security alerts after about a week. The Service's own warning and error log is kept until it reaches a set size, then replaced.
- **Backups**: daily snapshots of the file storage are kept for 14 days, and monthly backups of the whole Service for up to 14 months. Data you delete, or that we delete when you close your account, may stay in those backups until they expire. We restore from backups only to recover from a failure.
- **Closing your account**: when you ask us to delete your account, we delete your files, calendars, contacts and other content within one month, apart from what remains in backups as described above.

## 4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your data or share it for advertising. The Service runs on **our own self-hosted servers in the United Kingdom**, including the Office and virus-scanning parts. It shares data with others only in these cases:

- **People you share with**: users and guests you share files, calendars or conversations with, and anyone who has a public link you create.
- **Other Nextcloud servers**, only if you share with, or accept a share from, a user on another server ("federated sharing").
- **Google (Gmail)**, which delivers the emails the Service sends you, such as share and calendar notifications.
- **Nextcloud GmbH**, which runs:
  - the push service that delivers notifications to the Nextcloud mobile apps, if you use them. It receives a device identifier and an encrypted notification, not your files;
  - a public "STUN" server used when you start a Talk call. It sees your IP address, so that calls can connect. In a call, your device may connect directly to the other participants' devices, so they can also see your IP address.

  [Nextcloud's privacy policy](https://nextcloud.com/privacy/).
- **MET Norway and OpenStreetMap**, only if you set a location in the Dashboard's weather widget: that location is sent to them to look up the forecast ([MET Norway](https://www.met.no/en/About-us/privacy), [OpenStreetMap](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy)).

We do not publish your profile to Nextcloud's global address book, and we do not send usage statistics to Nextcloud.

## 5. Security

- Data is stored on servers we control. Connections to the Service are encrypted (HTTPS).
- Files are **not** end-to-end encrypted, and they are not encrypted at rest by the Service. Our administrators can technically access stored data. They do so only when needed to run, secure or repair the Service, or when the law requires it.
- You can protect your account with two-factor authentication in your sign-in account.

## 6. Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you can ask us to:

- Give you a copy of your data (you can also download your files, calendars and contacts yourself at any time)
- Correct or delete your data
- Restrict or object to how we use it

Email [{{ site.contact_email }}](mailto:{{ site.contact_email }}) from the address on your account, or tell us your username. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the [Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/).

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.

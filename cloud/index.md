Zero-Technologies Cloud, at [cloud.zero-technologies.co.uk](https://cloud.zero-technologies.co.uk), is a private file, calendar and collaboration service. Zero-Technologies runs it on its own servers in the United Kingdom, using the open-source [Nextcloud](https://nextcloud.com) software.

It is available by invitation to family, friends and members of our Patreon on a paid tier. You sign in with your [Zero-Technologies Sign-in](/sign-in/) account.

## What it includes

- **Files**: store, sync and share files, with the desktop and mobile apps
- **Calendar and Contacts**, which sync with your phone and computer
- **Office**: edit documents, spreadsheets and presentations in your browser (Collabora Online, hosted by us at `office.zero-technologies.co.uk`)
- **Whiteboard**: shared drawing boards
- **Talk**: chat and calls with other users

Each account has a storage quota, normally 15 GB. Uploaded files are checked for viruses.

The Authentik Discord app lets you sign in to Zero-Technologies services using your Discord account.

## How sign-in works

1. On a Zero-Technologies login page, choose **Sign in with Discord**.
2. Discord asks you to approve access to basic account information.
3. You are signed in to the service.

You can remove access at any time in Discord under **User Settings > Authorized Apps**.

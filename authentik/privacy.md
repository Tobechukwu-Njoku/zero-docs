This policy explains what data we collect when you sign in with the Authentik Discord app ("the App"), why, and what rights you have. Zero-Technologies ("we", "us") is the data controller.

## 1. Data we collect

When you approve the App, Discord shares the following with us, based on the permissions shown on the approval screen:

| Data | Discord scope | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Discord user ID, username and avatar | `identify` | To create and recognise your account |
| Email address | `email` | To contact you about your account |
| List of servers you are in | `guilds` | To check you are a member of an allowed server |

We also store sign-in logs (time, IP address and browser) to keep accounts secure.

We never receive your Discord password.

## 2. Legal basis

We process this data to provide the sign-in service you asked for (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)) and, for security logs, on the basis of legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)).

## 3. How long we keep it

- Account data is kept while your account exists, and deleted when you ask us to remove it.
- Sign-in logs are kept for [90] days, then deleted.

## 4. Who we share it with

We do not sell or share your data. It is processed by:

- **Discord**, which handles the sign-in ([Discord Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy))
- Our own self-hosted servers in the United Kingdom

## 5. Security

Data is stored on servers we control, with access limited to administrators.

## 6. Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you can ask us to:

- Give you a copy of your data
- Correct or delete your data
- Restrict or object to how we use it

Email [{{ site.contact_email }}](mailto:{{ site.contact_email }}) with your Discord user ID. We will reply within one month.

You can also remove the App's access at any time in Discord under **User Settings > Authorized Apps**.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the [Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/).

## 7. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.

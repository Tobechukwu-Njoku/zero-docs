Zero-Technologies Sign-in is the account and login service for Zero-Technologies services, at `auth.zero-technologies.co.uk`.

## Ways to sign in

- **Username and password.** Sign up with your email address; we send you a link to confirm it.
- **Discord, Google, Microsoft or Patreon.** Choose the provider's button on the login page and approve access to your basic account information. The first time, you pick a username and your account is created.

If you already have an account, you can connect Discord, Google, Microsoft or Patreon to it under **Settings > Connected services** after signing in.

## Removing access

- Disconnect a provider in **Settings > Connected services**.
- Discord: **User Settings > Authorized Apps**. Google: [Third-party connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections). Microsoft: [Apps and services](https://account.live.com/consent/Manage). Patreon: [Connected apps](https://www.patreon.com/settings/apps).
- To delete your account, see the [Privacy Policy](/sign-in/privacy/#6-your-rights).

Victor Fries is a Discord bot that sends you notifications from Silo, the Zero-Technologies media server, by direct message: for example new episodes of shows you follow, titles on your watchlist becoming available, and updates on your requests.

## Using Victor Fries

- Link your Discord account in your Silo notification settings, and choose which notifications you want and whether they arrive one by one or as a digest.
- Make sure Discord allows direct messages from the bot, or it cannot reach you.
- To stop notifications, turn them off or unlink Discord in your Silo notification settings. You can also remove the bot's access in Discord under **User Settings > Authorized Apps**.

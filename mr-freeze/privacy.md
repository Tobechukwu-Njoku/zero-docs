This policy explains what data the Mr. Freeze Discord bot ("the Bot") collects, why, and what rights you have. Zero-Technologies ("we", "us") is the data controller.

## 1. Data we collect

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Discord user ID and username | To identify you and send you notifications |
| Link between your Discord account and your account on our request service | To match requests to you |
| Request details (for example, title and status) | To include in the notification |
| Server and channel IDs | To deliver notifications to the right place |

We do not read your messages, and we do not collect your email address, IP address or payment details through the Bot.

## 2. Legal basis

We process this data on the basis of legitimate interests (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)): delivering the notifications you asked for.

## 3. How long we keep it

- Account links are kept until you unlink your account or ask us to delete them.
- Notification logs are kept for [30] days, then deleted.

## 4. Who we share it with

We do not sell or share your data. It is processed by:

- **Discord**, to deliver messages ([Discord Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy))
- Our own self-hosted servers in the United Kingdom

## 5. Security

Data is stored on servers we control, with access limited to administrators.

## 6. Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you can ask us to:

- Give you a copy of your data
- Correct or delete your data
- Restrict or object to how we use it

Email [{{ site.contact_email }}](mailto:{{ site.contact_email }}) with your Discord user ID. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the [Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/).

## 7. Children

The Bot is not intended for anyone under the minimum age set by Discord's Terms of Service.

## 8. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
